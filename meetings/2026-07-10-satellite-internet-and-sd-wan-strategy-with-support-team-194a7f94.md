### Company and Product Scope

- ConnectSat is the parent company, covering satellite phones, data terminals, and internet services
- Satellite phone store is under the same umbrella, not a separate entity
- Key internet providers sold: Starlink (most popular), Iridium, OneWeb, Viasat
- Peplink (SD-WAN/cellular router) is the current focus product for growth
  - ConnectSat is an authorized Peplink reseller, listed on Peplink’s website
  - CEO confirmed SD-WAN is a priority: higher margins than Starlink alone

### Target Industries and Customer Profile

- No single industry focus: military, oil and gas, telecom, hospitality, maritime, enterprise
- Britt’s directive: target enterprises with 500–1,000+ employees
- Largest use-case segment: backup and contingency communications
  - Companies need a fallback when primary internet or comms go down
- Hospitality is a strong vertical: hotels buy individually per property, rarely consolidate

### Core Pain Points and Solutions

- Primary pain point: unreliable internet causing downtime, buffering, dropped calls
- Peplink solves this via multi-WAN SD-WAN routing:
  - Combines fiber, 5G, Starlink, cellular into one bonded connection
  - Automatic failover with zero downtime, no manual intervention
  - Can fill gaps in a fiber connection in real time using Starlink
  - Example: customer bonding 8 Starlinks via one Peplink router, achieving 1 Gbps+
- Upsell bundle opportunity: Starlink + Peplink + battery backup in one transaction
- Remote/out-of-band management: cellular SIM in Peplink allows router access even if primary internet is down
