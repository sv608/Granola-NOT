# Email Campaign Strategy

- Sequences not yet complete (2-day gaps between emails), so results pending
- CTAs already updated in the last two emails
- Key principle: email alone won’t close deals; warm leads must be moved to LinkedIn and ads
  - HubSpot example: 6+6+6 email sequence, then 5-6 LinkedIn touches before getting a response
- Campaign naming: use persona name (e.g. “SEO Agencies”), not dates, matched exactly across SmartLead and LinkedIn
- LinkedIn sequence should read as a continuation of email, not a fresh intro

# Webinar Outreach (Indian SEO Agencies)

- Target: \~3,000 contacts (one-third of \~10,000 Indian SEO agency list) to avoid burning the full list
- Infrastructure: custom SMTP provider instead of SmartLead
  - SmartLead: ₹4.5/inbox, capped at 10-20 emails/inbox, expensive at scale
  - Custom SMTP: \~₹1,000 for 14,000 emails/month, unlimited mailboxes, tracks opens and clicks
  - Warmed-up domains already in place
- Campaign goal: drive webinar registrations; all 5-6 emails focused solely on that
- Follow-up campaign for opens who didn’t register: share recording, pitch next webinar in the series
- Panel member angle: invite SEO agency founders to join as panelists for visibility; run as a separate sequence
- Current signups: 4, including some via personal LinkedIn outreach
- LinkedIn groups: plan to post webinar signup there too
