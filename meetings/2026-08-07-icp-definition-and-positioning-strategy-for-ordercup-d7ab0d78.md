# ICP and Positioning Angles

- Customer distribution is mixed: Amazon-only, Shopify-only, and multi-channel (Amazon + eBay + Shopify + Walmart)
- Key insight: FBA-only sellers are not a fit (Amazon handles fulfillment, no domain, no need for OrderCup)
- Sweet spot: FBA + FBM hybrid sellers who also have D2C stores
  - Growing segment: was 12%, now 20–25% of Amazon sellers
  - Typically follow 80/20 rule: fast movers go FBA, slow movers go FBM
  - These sellers won’t ask “why not just use Shopify shipping?”
- Churn reasons identified:
  - Outgrown the platform (Enterprise customers needing ERP integrations)
  - Shopify-native shipping sufficient for simple, low-volume sellers
  - Cost and UI complaints
- Three positioning angles emerging:
  1. FBA + FBM hybrid + D2C multi-channel: better profitability and efficiency vs. pure FBA
  2. Small 3PL providers: lack sophisticated WMS, could use OrderCup (current Boston-based partner has 17–18 Shopify stores on OrderCup)
  3. Brand-conscious merchants avoiding Amazon’s customer data lock-in and branded packaging
