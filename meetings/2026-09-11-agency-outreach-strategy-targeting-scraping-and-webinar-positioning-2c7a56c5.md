# Target Market: Geography and Agency Profile

- Start with less competitive, less digitally savvy US states
  - Priority targets: Iowa, North Carolina, Tennessee
  - Avoid major metros: New York, Chicago, San Francisco, Austin (LA still viable)
  - Rationale: restrains ad budget, less saturated
- Target agency profile: small SEO/digital agencies (under 10 people)
  - Cater to local service businesses: plumbers, dentists, HR firms
  - Offer full-service: SEO, PPC, social
  - Example: True SEO Solutions, True SEO Digital

# Data Scraping and Lead Generation

- Running scrape via Outscraper (Google Maps listings)
  - Currently pulling Iowa and North Carolina as sample run
  - Will return contact details for qualifying agencies
- Outreach strategy: go direct to founders
  - Small agencies = founders easy to reach via LinkedIn
  - Note: many have incomplete or missing LinkedIn profiles (“that’s how bad they are”)
- Additional channel: webinars to bring agencies in

# Campaign Channels and Approach

- Lead with ads, layer email marketing on top
- Geo-target ads to selected states to control spend
- Keyword strategy: focus on what these small agencies are actually searching for

# Landing Page and Offer

- Offer: 15-day free trial, then one month free
- Needs clear positioning in:
  - Hero section CTA
  - Final CTA (last section)
- Subham to ensure offer copy is written clearly and correctly
