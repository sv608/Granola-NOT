# CSM role requirements — strategy execution, quality output judgment, and customer delivery

### CSM Role Requirements

- Strategy execution capability
  - Must understand KPIs and expected outcomes
  - Break down strategy into daily actionable tasks
  - Create and maintain daily update plans
  - Map customer visit outcomes and results
- Design taste and quality assessment
  - Recognize good vs bad output quality
  - Have patience to review deliverables thoroughly
  - Provide feedback on creative and design elements
- Basic marketing understanding
  - Know what USPs are and how to generate them
  - Work with experts like content creators
  - Understand landing page frameworks and structure
  - Use AI tools (ChatGPT) effectively with knowledge base

### Current Team Performance Issues

- Execution problems with existing hires
  - Can’t create proper landing page frameworks
  - Don’t understand basic redirect creation process
  - Missing client management experience and common sense
  - Poor output quality - “pure crap” deliverables
- Age and learning challenges
  - People in late 30s-50s have “lost it”
  - Don’t want to learn new things or adapt
  - Lack mindset for continuous learning
  - Missing basic common sense in execution
- Delivery engine breakdown
  - No proper business delivery system
  - Quality output “absolutely terrible”
  - Client confidence issues emerging

### Ideal Candidate Profile

- Demographics: Mid-20s (25-27 years old) from tier 2 cities
- Core competencies needed:
  1. At least one expertise area: SEO, email marketing, or content
  2. Common sense and trainability
  3. Independent client call management
  4. Confidence in customer interactions
- Mental agility requirements
  - Quick learning and adaptation
  - Ability to run with strategies independently
  - Problem-solving mindset

### Assignment Framework for Candidates

- CSM newsletter setup (30-40 minutes daily)
- Data file management for newsletters
  - Find most relevant audience segments
  - Set up campaigns and validation processes
  - Handle campaign management operations
- Webinar coordination skills
  - Learn complete webinar management
  - Integrate into overall strategy

### Client Strategy Assignment Test

- Provided materials:
  - Full quarter strategy document
  - Business transcripts and knowledge base
  - Sales pitch materials and website data
  - Four major positioning wedges
- Required deliverables:
  1. One complete landing page for chosen wedge
  2. One non-AI newsletter email
  3. Content calendar with 3-4 blog recommendations
- Support available:
  - Can ask subject matter experts questions
  - Access to AI tools and free accounts
  - Full documentation and input materials provided

### Outbound Strategy Implementation

- Data cleanup process
  - Manual list building: 50-200 targeted people
  - Use ChatGPT, Clay, or similar tools
  - Validate ICP through Perplexity
- Campaign execution
  1. Generate personalized content for 50 people
  2. Send initial outreach
  3. Monitor open and response rates
  4. Analyze what’s working before scaling
- Data validation requirements
  - LinkedIn profile verification
  - Company designation confirmation
  - Signal harvesting for personalization
