### The Idea: Deploy (AI Talent Platform)

- “Marketer Hire, but for AI”: a vetted marketplace connecting Western companies with AI talent from India, Pakistan, Bangladesh
- Core gap: no platform specializes in AI roles; Upwork/Fiverr are unvetted and low-trust; Marketer Hire covers only marketing
- Starting niche: forward deployed engineers (high LinkedIn job volume in the US right now)
- Expand later into AI marketing, AI HR, tool-specific specialists (HubSpot AI, Salesforce AI, etc.)
- Long-term vision: become the TCS/Infosys of AI

### Business Model and Competitive Edge

- Free for talent, charge only clients on contract placements (no subscriptions)
- Target 50% margin (similar to Marketer Hire’s 2x model), justified by full service wrap:
  - Employer of record, tax management, bi-weekly auto-pay to talent
  - Reference checks, background verification, degree validation
  - Complaint handling, replacement guarantee (no refund, replace for free)
  - Scoring system: complaints reduce match rate, skills additions improve it
- Contract model preferred over freelance (reliability, fixed hours)
- Controlled onboarding: application-based intake, AI pre-vetting before interviews
  - Goal: 500 to 1,000 highly qualified people in the pool before opening broadly
  - Prevents cloud cost blowout from unrestricted open signups

### Go-to-Market Plan

- Talent acquisition (Subham leads):
  - LinkedIn organic + ads (cheap in India, even 10-50 INR/day)
  - Partner with AI training academies and agencies (Mumbai etc.), distribute brochures
  - Outreach via Upwork/Fiverr AI sections, invite talent to register
  - Subham’s 25K LinkedIn following as a direct talent funnel
  - Visit IITs and colleges to recruit
- Client acquisition (other co-founder leads, Canada-facing):
  - LinkedIn outbound to companies actively posting AI/forward deploy roles
  - Pitch: free two-week trial, no cost to try, pre-vetted pool ready
  - No sales hire needed yet; Canada-based co-founder handles trust and calls
- Platform UX goal: employer creates a pain-point brief, gets matched profiles, talks directly to talent (no intermediary except for enterprise deals)

### Co-Founder Structure and Roles

- Three-way split preferred over a small equity slice for the tech co-founder
  - Risk of low-equity founder disengaging or becoming adversarial once revenue flows
  - Tech co-founder must be fully committed, not just a contractor
- Role split:
  - Canada co-founder: client-facing, sales, trust-building, North American market
  - Subham: execution, India-side ops, agency relationships, talent sourcing, marketing
  - Tech co-founder (TBD): platform architecture, infrastructure, backend
- “Wai” explicitly ruled out (wrong mindset for equity-based long-term commitment)
- Subham to identify a candidate from India; both will search
- Subham’s contact (42-year-old MNC employee) noted as a possible lead but likely time-constrained

### Platform, Costs, and Company Setup

- Platform approach: prototype first, iterate fast, target launch within one month
  - Frontend: web app (Lovable suggested)
  - Backend: needs a real engineer for infra, DB, deployment, token management
  - AI agents to handle vetting, matching, scoring (no human ops team initially)
- Bootstrapped: count every rupee, no unnecessary hires, shared investment
  - Main costs: platform build, cloud/token spend, LinkedIn ads, tools
  - Intern in India (\~10,000 INR/month) for LinkedIn outbound support is acceptable
- Company registration: both Canada and India as a joint venture
  - Canada entity for enterprise client trust
  - India entity for tax efficiency
  - Will consult on optimal HQ + branch structure
