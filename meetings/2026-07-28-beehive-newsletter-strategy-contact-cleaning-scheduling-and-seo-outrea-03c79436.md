### Beehive Newsletter Cadence

- Send two newsletters per week: Monday (business) and Thursday (technical)
  - Content already structured in G Drive newsletter folder, week by week
  - Each week has two articles; images in the charts/images folder
- Schedule 3-6 weeks of newsletters in one session, not piecemeal
- Contact list hygiene rules (all three apply, not optional):
  1. Remove unsubscribed contacts immediately
  2. Remove bounced contacts
  3. Remove contacts who haven’t opened any newsletter in 2-3 weeks
- No sequencing needed: send all active contacts the current week’s newsletter each time
